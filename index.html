The Kitchen

One hearth, no line cooks.

There are four people in the kitchen on any given night, and all four are standing at the same fire. Ember doesn't run a line — it runs a hearth, the way it was cooked before kitchens had corners.

Chef Mireille Okonkwo built the restaurant around a single custom-forged grill that can hold coal at nine different heights, and most nights, she's the one moving the food between them.
