The Kitchen

One hearth, no line cooks.

There are four people in the kitchen on any given night, and all four are standing at the same fire. Ember doesn't run a line — it runs a hearth, the way it was cooked before kitchens had corners.

Chef Mireille Okonkwo built the restaurant around a single custom-forged grill that can hold coal at nine different heights, and most nights, she's the one moving the food between them.

"I don't measure heat in degrees. I measure it in how far the coal is from the fish, and how long since it was split."

Mireille Okonkwo
Chef & Owner, Ember

The Cellar

Four hundred bottles, chosen for smoke.

Our sommelier builds the pairing list around what survives contact with char — structured reds, skin-contact whites, and a short list of sherries most restaurants won't touch.

Pairing flight runs alongside all eight courses, poured by the glass, never pre-batched.
